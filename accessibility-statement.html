Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Keizer Mobile Detailing is committed to providing a website that can be used by people with disabilities and to offering reasonable alternative ways to obtain information or request service.

Our accessibility goal

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA where reasonably possible. WCAG provides guidance for making web content perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.

Measures we take

  • Use semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and meaningful alternative text.
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible keyboard focus.
  • Manage focus when dialogs open and close.
  • Provide accessible form labels, instructions, validation, and status messages.
  • Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid automatically playing video clips.
  • Use responsive layouts and readable color contrast.
  • Review new and updated content for accessibility issues.

Known limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and some third-party tools, embedded services, older media, or content outside our direct control may not fully meet our accessibility goal. We work to provide an alternative when a barrier is reported.

Need assistance or found a problem?

If you have difficulty using this website, need information in another format, or need help requesting a quote or service, use the Contact Us form or call or text (971) 286-5503. Please include the page or feature involved and a brief description of the problem.

We will make a reasonable effort to respond and provide an accessible alternative.

Ongoing review

This statement and the website were most recently reviewed on July 11, 2026. We may update this statement as the website, technology, and accessibility guidance change.

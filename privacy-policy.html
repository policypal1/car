Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keizer Mobile Detailing collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website, request a quote, contact us, or schedule a service.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information that you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, message, vehicle information, service location, appointment details, and photos or other information you send for a quote.

When you use the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as your Internet Protocol address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, interactions with the website, approximate location derived from your Internet Protocol address, and the date and time of your visit.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to questions and quote requests.
  • To schedule, provide, and follow up on detailing services.
  • To communicate about appointments, service changes, and customer support.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • To understand website traffic and marketing performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our customers, business, and rights.

3. Analytics, cookies, and similar technologies

This website may use Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights, and similar technologies to measure website usage and performance. These providers may receive technical information about your browser, device, and interactions with the website. Your browser may allow you to block or delete cookies. Blocking certain technologies may affect website features or measurement.

4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information to vendors that help us host the website, process form submissions, provide analytics, communicate with customers, or support business operations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a business transfer.

We do not knowingly sell personal information for money, and we do not use this website for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with law. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and why it was collected.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also ask questions about how information is used or withdraw consent to future non-required communications.

To make a request, use the Contact Us form on the website or call or text (971) 286-5503. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

9. Third-party links

The website may link to third-party services, including Instagram, Google, and review or scheduling services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when the latest revision was posted.

11. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests may be submitted through the website’s Contact Us form or by calling or texting (971) 286-5503.
